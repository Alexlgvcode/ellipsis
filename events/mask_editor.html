    How to

    • Select a zone: click it on the image or in the list.
    • Move a point: drag a square handle. ←↑→↓ nudge the last point by 1 px (Shift = 5 px).
    • Add a point: drag a round handle in the middle of an edge.
    • Remove a point: Alt/⌥-click it.
    • New zone: pick a type, click + New zone, click the corners, then click the first point or press Enter. Esc cancels.
    • Undo: ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z.
    • Drag the slider to check the mask on other recorded frames.
    • Coordinates are in 352x240 frame pixels; the image is shown 3x.
